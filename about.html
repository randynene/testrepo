Why we exist

Hiring budgets are the last unmeasured line in marketing

Most companies can tell you the return on a product campaign to the euro. Ask the same question about the job board package renewed every January and the answer is a shrug. The money is spent, the roles are eventually filled, and nobody connects the two.

We think that gap is the whole opportunity. When you can see which channel produced a hire and what it cost, the obvious decisions become available: move budget, kill the channel that only produces volume, write the ad that speaks to the person you actually want.

That is the only thing we sell. The employer brand work, the media buying, the landing pages and the reporting all exist to make one number visible and then make it fall: cost per qualified applicant.

How we work

Five things we hold to

These are the positions we will not trade away for a bigger retainer. Two of them have cost us work.

We show the raw numbers

You get dashboard access on day one, including the weeks where the figures are bad. No screenshots pasted into a monthly deck.

The people who pitch do the work

Four named people on your account. Nobody senior sells the engagement and then hands it to someone you have never met.

We write what your team says

Every proposition comes out of interviews with your own people. If they would not say it in a screening call, it does not ship.

We say no to volume briefs

If the goal is application count rather than hires, we are the wrong agency and we will tell you in the first call.

You keep everything

Ad accounts, pages, sequences and source files are yours, in your own tenancies. Leaving us should cost you nothing but notice.

Story

How the agency got here

2019
Two people, one plant

Anna and Jonah took on a manufacturer that could not fill twelve shift roles. Paid social replaced three job boards and the cost per hire fell by a figure nobody believed at first.

2021
Creative comes in house

Sofia joined and we stopped subcontracting film. Employee stories shot on site turned out to outperform anything scripted, and the studio moved to Kreuzberg.

2023
Attribution, properly

Liam built the reporting layer that ties an impression to a signed offer. It became the reason most clients stay, and the reason we can price on outcomes.

2026
Twelve people, nine markets

Still one office, still no account managers between you and the work. We take on roughly eight clients a year and turn down more than that.

In practice

What working with us actually looks like

One weekly call

Thirty minutes, same slot, media lead and strategy lead present. Budget decisions get made on the call rather than in a follow-up thread.

A shared channel

Slack or Teams, whichever you already use. Recruiters ask for a new ad variant directly, without routing through a project manager.

A written monthly read

Two pages: what moved, what we changed, what we would stop. Sent before the meeting so the meeting can be about decisions.

Notice, not lock-in

Retainers run month to month after the first quarter, on thirty days' notice. We would rather earn the renewal than hold a contract.
